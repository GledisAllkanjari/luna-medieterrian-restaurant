WHERE IT STARTED

From a small idea to a place of our own.

Luna Bistro opened its doors in Tirana in 2018 with a simple purpose: create a restaurant where great food and genuine hospitality come together.

Inspired by the Mediterranean, our kitchen focuses on fresh ingredients, traditional techniques and modern presentation.

But Luna is about more than what is on the plate. It is about long dinners, spontaneous conversations, celebrations and the people sitting around the table.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Less noise.
More flavor.

We believe food doesn't need to be complicated to be extraordinary.

Our chefs work with seasonal ingredients and let their natural character lead the dish. Every plate is carefully considered, but never overworked.

The result is food that feels familiar, contemporary and unmistakably Mediterranean.
